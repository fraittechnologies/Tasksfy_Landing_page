How Tasksfy Works

Download the tasksfy app from the playstore, create an account, choose your preferred tasker for your task, get a work quotation, supervise the work and rate the tasker.

1

Service Request

The client requests a service from a tasker of their choice, including images describing the task ahead.

2

Work Quotation

The tasker accepts the task request and, based on the image description, sends a work quotation to the client.

3

Verification of Payment

The client secures the agreed amount in an escrow account, and payment is verified for the commencement of the task.

4

Task supervision

The client supervises the task being carried out by the tasker.

Pay & Rate

5

The client marks the task as complete and both parties rate each other based on the task. Payment is then processed to the taskers account.
